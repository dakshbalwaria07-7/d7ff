❓ App Tutorial

Step 1: Sign up with your email & password.

Step 2: Go to My Profile and add your Free Fire UID and WhatsApp number.

Step 3: Add money to your wallet using UPI.

Step 4: Choose a game and join any tournament.

Step 5: Select your team position (for Duo/Squad).

Step 6: Play match, win prizes!

Step 7: Withdraw your winnings to your UPI.
